What Are Flock Cameras?

Flock cameras are a network of high-speed, computer-controlled cameras that automatically read and record license plate information from passing vehicles. They do not keep you safer. They track and store massive amounts of data about you. They are used to stalk and harass, with no oversight, and little internal auditing.

Flock cameras, and other ALPRs like them do not help keep you safe. Here are some real world examples of how this data is being used:

  • Stalking of exes, girlfriends, and other individuals.

  • Hunting a woman across the state because she got an abortion.

  • Sharing the info with DHS and ICE.
